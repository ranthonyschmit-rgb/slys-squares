How to play. Drag across the grid to draw a box. Every box must hold exactly one number, and that number must equal how many squares the box covers. Fill all 104 squares to finish.

Tap a box to remove it. Drawing over existing boxes replaces them. A correct box fills with the color for its size. A box with too many squares or two numbers gets striped.
